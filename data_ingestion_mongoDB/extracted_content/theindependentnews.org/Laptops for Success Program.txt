Title: Laptops for Success Program
URL: https://theindependentnews.org/2025/01/25/laptops-for-success-program/

Need a laptop that fits your student budget? Thanks to a partnership between Community College of Philadelphia (CCP) and PCs for People, you can get a refurbished laptop for as little as $25—or $50 if you don’t qualify for certain financial aid.
Every laptop comes with one year of technical support for both hardware and any pre-installed software, like Microsoft Word or PowerPoint.
If you run into any problems with your computer or the purchasing process, CCP’s IT Services team is ready to assist:
Location: Bonnell Building, Room B2-41
Phone: 215-496-6000, ext. 6000
Email: 4ITSupport@ccp.edu
Ready to get started?
Visit https://www.pcsforpeople.org/ccpcomputers/ to claim your laptop today, or visit CCP’s Old Website
